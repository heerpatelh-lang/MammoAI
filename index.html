📋 Questions for Your Doctor

Tailored to your specific assessment indicators.

MammoAI operates as an open-box, rule-based demonstration system. Here is the exact pipeline that processed your indicators:

User Inputs (Age, Lump, Density, Calcification) ↓ Indicator Normalization & Weights Lookup ↓ Rule-Based Demonstration Model (Summation) ↓ Concern Categorization Thresholds (<2.4: Lower, 2.5-5.4: Moderate, >5.4: Higher) ↓ Dynamic Natural Language Explanation Generator ↓ Personalized Next-Step & Doctor Question Generator

Notice for judges & educators: The demonstration weights are configured in centralized configuration (`MODEL_CONFIG.weights`). Indicators marked as Unclear / Not reported are never assumed to be negative or positive; they are explicitly flagged for clarification. This model is purely educational and has not been clinically validated.

About MammoAI

Promoting early understanding and responsible AI in healthcare education.

Our Mission

Breast screening can feel intimidating, filled with dense medical jargon and ambiguous reports. MammoAI was built to make mammogram-related information easier to understand through transparent, responsible AI-assisted technology.

Our core philosophy: MammoAI doesn't just give a result. It helps users understand it and know what to do next.

Why Explainability Matters

In modern healthcare AI, a "black-box" prediction that simply outputs a percentage creates anxiety rather than empowerment. Explainability transforms algorithms into educational partners: users deserve to see which specific factors influenced a model, what those factors mean clinically, and how to verify them with a qualified physician.

Prototype Limitations & Safety

MammoAI is a student educational prototype and adheres to strict medical boundaries:

  • It is NOT a medical device and is NOT clinically validated.
  • It cannot determine whether cancer is present or absent.
  • It does NOT provide actual cancer probabilities or prescribe treatments.
  • Missing or unclear report indicators are never treated as negative confirmations.

Privacy & Data Control

MammoAI values privacy. We do not require accounts, logins, emails, names, or patient IDs. All assessment information is kept in your private browser session.
